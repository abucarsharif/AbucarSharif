Manchester

Live in Manchester

Manchester is a major industrial city in the north-west of England. It has two major football teams, Manchester United and Manchester City. People like Manchester for its art, music and food.

Manchester is home to three large and famous universities:

  • The University of Manchester
  • Manchester Metropolitian University
  • The University of Salford

Manchester is a fantastic city! It is full of beautiful buildings and fun things. Here are the top 3 places to visit in Manchester

  1. The John Rylands Library
  2. Science and Industry Museum
  3. National Football Museum

If you need more information about Manchester, you can visit Manchester City Council
